CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.3162: 

450.3162 Sec. 1162.In the event of an amendment to the articles of incorporation or bylaws, merger, or disposition of substantially all of the assets of a cooperative, or a dissolution, that results in a distribution of all or substantially all of the assets of the corporation to members, the corporation shall make that distribution in the manner and order provided in section 1183.
