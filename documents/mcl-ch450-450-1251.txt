CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1251: 

450.1251 Sec. 251.(1)A corporation may be formed under this act for any lawful purpose, except to engage in a business for which a corporation may be formed under any other statute of this state unless that statute permits formation under this act.(2)In time of war or other national emergency, a corporation may take any lawful action to provide aid, notwithstanding the purposes set forth in its articles of incorporation, at the request or direction of a competent governmental authority.
