CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2067: 

125.2067 Sec. 67.A certified minority venture capital company and a certified MESBIC shall have an independent program audit annually performed by a certified public accountant. The fund may visit and examine a certified minority venture capital company or a certified MESBIC and may delegate the authority to visit and examine to the commissioner of the financial institutions bureau.
