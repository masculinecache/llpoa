CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.174: 

559.174 Sec. 74.(1) The condominium subdivision plan of a size as provided by rule of the administrator shall be delivered to and retained by the local register of deeds office.(2)A consolidating master deed shall be recorded at the register of deeds office. The register of deeds shall not deny recording of a consolidating master deed because the property taxes and special assessments are not paid in full.
