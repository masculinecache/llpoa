CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.172: 

559.172 Sec. 72.(1) A condominium project for any property shall be established upon the recording of a master deed that complies with this act.(2)Except as provided in section 88, a condominium unit shall not be sold by or on behalf of the developer before a master deed is recorded for the condominium units in the project.(3)Title to a unit and common elements is not rendered unmarketable or otherwise affected by reason of an insubstantial failure of the master deed to comply with this act. Whether a substantial failure of the master deed to comply with this act impairs marketability is not affected by this subsection.
