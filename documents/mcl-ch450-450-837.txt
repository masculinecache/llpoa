CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.837: 

450.837 Sec. 7.(1)A record or signature shall not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because it is in electronic form.(2)A contract shall not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because an electronic record was used in its formation.(3)If a law requires a record to be in writing, an electronic record satisfies the law.(4)If a law requires a signature, an electronic signature satisfies the law.
