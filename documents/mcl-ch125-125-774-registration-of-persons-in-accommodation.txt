CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.774: REGISTRATION OF PERSONS IN ACCOMMODATIONS FOR TRANSIENTS

125.774 Sec. 4.This act shall take effect 6 months after it is signed by the governor or becomes law without the governor's signature. Act 75 of 1952 REGISTRATION OF PERSONS IN ACCOMMODATIONS FOR TRANSIENTS AN ACT to provide for the registration of persons in camps, tourist homes, tourist rooms, cabins, house trailer camps and other sleeping accommodations for transients; and to provide penalties for violation of the provisions of this act.
