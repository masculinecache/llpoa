17.2 MULTIPLE DWELLINGS - BULK, DENSITY AND AREA
Minimum floor area per each unit
Efficiency 250 sq. ft.
One (1) bedroom 400 sq. ft.
Two (2) bedroom 500 sq. ft.

Lot size
Minimum width 200 ft. at front building line
Minimum 40,000 sq. ft. for any
combination of six (6) bedrooms
For every bedroom over six (6), add 1,000 sq.
ft. to the minimum lot size

Three (3) bedroom 600 sq. ft.
Multiple Dwellings require a County Health Department written approval and/or permit for all proposed or
installed septic tanks and wells. For the purpose of applying yard regulation, multiple-family dwellings shall
be considered as one (1) building occupying one (1) lot. When more than one (1) multiple-dwelling building
occupies one (1) lot, the structures must be separated by at least thirty feet (30’) when end to end, sixty feet
(60’) when back to back or face to face, and forty feet (40’) when end to face or back.
