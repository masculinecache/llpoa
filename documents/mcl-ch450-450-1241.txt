CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1241: 

450.1241 Sec. 241.(1) Each domestic corporation and each foreign corporation authorized to transact business in this state shall have and continuously maintain in this state both of the following:(a) A registered office, which may be the same as its place of business.(b) A resident agent. A resident agent may be an individual resident of this state; a domestic corporation or limited liability company; or a foreign corporation or limited liability company authorized to transact business in this state. (2) The address of the business office or residence of a resident agent must be the same as the address of the registered office.
