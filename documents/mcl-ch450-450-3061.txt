CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.3061: 

450.3061 Sec. 1061.Every corporation organized or conducting affairs in this state shall, upon filing its articles, or, if a foreign corporation, upon filing its application for admission, pay to the administrator a fee of $10.00 for the privilege of exercising its franchises within this state, upon such organization or admission as the case may be.
