CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.3011: 

450.3011 Sec. 1011.A foreign corporation shall not conduct affairs in this state until it has procured a certificate of authority so to do from the administrator. A foreign corporation may be authorized to conduct affairs in this state which may be conducted lawfully in this state by a domestic corporation, to the extent that it is authorized to conduct such affairs in the jurisdiction where it is organized, but no other affairs.
