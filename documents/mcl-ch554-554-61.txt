CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.61: 

554.61 Sec. 1.As used in this act:(a)"Terminable interest" is a possessory or ownership interest in real property which is subject to termination by a provision in a conveyance or other instrument which either creates a right of reversion to a grantor or his heirs, successors or assigns or creates a right of entry on the occurrence of a specified contingency.(b)"Specified contingency" is the event described in a conveyance or other instrument creating a terminable interest, the occurrence of which requires or permits the divesting of the terminable interest.
