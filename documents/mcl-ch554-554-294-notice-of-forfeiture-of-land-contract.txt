CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.294: NOTICE OF FORFEITURE OF LAND CONTRACT

554.294 Sec. 4.As used in this act, "person" means an individual, partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity. Act 235 of 1929 NOTICE OF FORFEITURE OF LAND CONTRACT AN ACT to provide for serving notice of forfeiture of land contracts upon vendees or others entitled to such notice when such persons are absent from the state or concealed therein, or when their whereabouts is unknown.
