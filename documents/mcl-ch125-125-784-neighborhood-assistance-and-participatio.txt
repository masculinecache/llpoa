CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.784: NEIGHBORHOOD ASSISTANCE AND PARTICIPATION ACT

125.784 Sec. 4.In order to assist in the protection of public welfare and safety, this act is enacted under the police power of the state. Act 56 of 1980 NEIGHBORHOOD ASSISTANCE AND PARTICIPATION ACT AN ACT to create a neighborhood assistance program; to prescribe the powers and duties of the department of labor; to create a fund; to permit certain rebates to business firms participating in neighborhood projects; and to require certain reports.
