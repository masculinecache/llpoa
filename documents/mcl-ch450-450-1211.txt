CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1211: 

450.1211 Sec. 211.Except as provided in chapter 2A for a professional corporation, the corporate name of a domestic corporation shall contain the word "corporation", "company", "incorporated", or "limited" or shall contain 1 of the following abbreviations: corp., co., inc., or ltd., with or without periods.
