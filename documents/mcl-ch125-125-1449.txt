CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1449: 

125.1449 Sec. 49.Every mortgage of real estate held by the authority which contains a power of sale, upon default being made in any condition of such mortgage, may be foreclosed by advertisement, in the cases and in the manner specified in sections 49a to 49v, including the giving of a notice as described in sections 49b and 49c.
