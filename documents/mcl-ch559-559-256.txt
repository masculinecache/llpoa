CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.256: 

559.256 Sec. 156.A person may not represent that the fact that an application under this act is filed or a permit is granted constitutes a finding by the administrator that a document filed under this act is true, complete, or not misleading. A person may not represent that the administrator passed upon the merits or qualifications of, or recommended or gave approval to, a person, developer, transaction, or condominium project.
