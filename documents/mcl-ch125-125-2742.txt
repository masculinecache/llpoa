CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2742: 

125.2742 Sec. 2.As used in this act:(a)"Applicant" means an individual and the spouse of that individual if that spouse intends to occupy the property with the individual.(b)"Local governmental unit" means a county, city, village, or township.(c)"Program" means the urban homesteading program for vacant land described in this act.(d)"Vacant property" means surplus vacant residential property owned by the local governmental unit.
