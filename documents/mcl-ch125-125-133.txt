CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.133: 

125.133 Sec. 3.As used in this act:(a) "Municipality" means a city, village, or township.(b) "Participating" means, with respect to a municipality, that the municipality is a member of a joint planning commission.(c) "Registered elector of the municipality" means a registered elector residing in the municipality or, if the municipality is a township, a registered elector residing in the portion of the township outside the limits of any village.
