CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1586: CONTRACTS FOR IMPROVEMENT TO REAL PROPERTY

125.1586 Sec. 16.The department shall submit to the senate and house committees that have the responsibility for economic development matters 2 reports covering the effectiveness of this act, the number of applicants who were accepted into business incubation centers, and the number of such businesses in operation at the time of the writing of each report. The first report shall be submitted before January 31, 1985, and the second report before January 31, 1990. Act 57 of 1998 CONTRACTS FOR IMPROVEMENT TO REAL PROPERTY AN ACT to require contractors to provide certain notices to governmental entities concerning improvements on real property; to allow for the modification of contracts for improvement to real property; to provide for remedies; and to repeal acts and parts of acts.
