CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.150: 

559.150 Sec. 50.If there is no co-owner other than the developer, the developer, with the consent of any interested mortgagee, may unilaterally terminate the condominium project or amend the master deed. A termination or amendment under this section shall become effective upon the recordation thereof if executed by the developer.
