CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.535: 

554.535 Sec. 15.(1)A transferor shall place the custodian in control of the custodial property as soon as practicable.(2)A transfer shall be made only for 1 minor, and only 1 person shall be the custodian. Custodial property held under this act by the same custodian for the benefit of the same minor constitutes a single custodianship.
