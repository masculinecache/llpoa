CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.554: 

125.554 Sec. 4.No person or persons, firm or corporation, shall supply with fuel oil any tanks or containers for fuel oil burners unless such fuel oil burners and equipment shall have been approved as provided herein and permit tag attached to the filler pipe of such tank or containers in the manner herein specified.
