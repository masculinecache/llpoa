CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.126: 

560.126 Sec. 126.(1)The survey of all subdivisions shall be performed by a surveyor.(2)The relative error of closure of the surveyed land shall be less than the ratio of 1 part in 5,000.(3)Bearings shall be expressed in relation to the true meridian, or a previously established meridian or bearing and a statement by the surveyor on the plat stating the source of information in obtaining the bearings outlined. 560.131.D10 FINAL PLATS
