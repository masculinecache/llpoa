CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1775: 

450.1775 Sec. 775.(1)For purposes of this chapter, the words and phrases in sections 776, 777, 778, and 779 have the meanings ascribed to them in those sections.(2)To the extent that the provisions of this chapter alter voting rights or are otherwise inconsistent with other provisions of this act, the provisions of this chapter shall control.
