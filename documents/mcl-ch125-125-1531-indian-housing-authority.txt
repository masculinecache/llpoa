CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1531: INDIAN HOUSING AUTHORITY

125.1531 Sec. 31.This act shall take effect January 1, 1973. Act 220 of 1979 INDIAN HOUSING AUTHORITY AN ACT to provide for the establishment of Indian housing authorities on Indian reservations in this state; and to prescribe the powers and duties of an Indian housing authority.
