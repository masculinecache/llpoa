CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.993: 

125.993 Sec. 3.After the effective date of this act, a new mobile home sold by a mobile home dealer situated in this state shall be covered by the warranty hereinafter specified. The warranty shall cover the purchaser of the mobile home and shall respectively apply to the manufacturer of the mobile home and to the dealer who sells the mobile home to the buyer in accordance with the terms of the warranty hereinafter specified.
