CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.5001: 

450.5001 Sec. 1001.Subject to the constitution of this state, the laws of the jurisdiction under which a foreign limited liability company is organized shall govern its organization and internal affairs, and a foreign limited liability company shall not be denied a certificate of authority to transact business in this state by reason of any difference between those laws and the laws of this state.
