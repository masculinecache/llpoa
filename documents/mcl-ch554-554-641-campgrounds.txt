CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.641: CAMPGROUNDS

554.641 Sec. 11.This act shall not take effect until July 1, 1979. Act 162 of 1990 CAMPGROUNDS AN ACT to provide certain remedies to owners and operators of certain campgrounds in case of disturbances, damage to property, nonpayment of rent, or unlawful presence on certain campgrounds; to allow for a lien in favor of campground owners and operators and to provide for its enforcement; and to prescribe certain powers of law enforcement officers.
