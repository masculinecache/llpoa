CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.5004: 

450.5004 Sec. 1004.The department shall not issue a certificate of authority to a foreign limited liability company unless the name of the company satisfies the requirements of section 204. If the name of a foreign limited liability company does not satisfy the requirements of section 204, the company may take the action authorized by section 204(4).
