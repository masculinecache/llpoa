CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.923: 

554.923 Sec. 23.(1) The fact that an application for registration has been filed or approved does not constitute any of the following:(a) Approval of or a finding regarding the accuracy of any information in or accompanying the registration application.(b) A recommendation, approval, or other finding by the department concerning the merits or qualifications of a person, life interest, long-term lease, transaction, or continuing care community.(2) A person shall not make or cause to be made to a prospective member a representation inconsistent with this section.
