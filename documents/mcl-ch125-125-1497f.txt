CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1497f: 

125.1497f Sec. 97f.As used in this chapter, the term "surplus" shall not be deemed to include any increase in assets of any mobile home park corporation organized in accordance with the provisions of this chapter, by reason of reduction of mortgage, by amortization or similar payments, or realized from the sale or disposition of any assets of a mobile home park corporation to the extent such surplus can be attributed to any increase in market value of any real property or tangible personal property accruing during the period the assets were owned and held by the mobile home park corporation. 125.1498.D10 Chapter
