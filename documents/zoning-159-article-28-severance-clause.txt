ARTICLE 28 SEVERANCE CLAUSE
Sections of this Ordinance shall be deemed to be severable and should any section, paragraph, or provision
hereof be declared by the courts to be unconstitutional or invalid, such holdings shall not affect the validity of
this ordinance as a whole or any part thereof, other than the part so declared to be unconstitutional or invalid.
