CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.3885: SIGNS ADVERTISING SEXUALLY ORIENTED BUSINESSES

125.3885 Sec. 85.(1) The following acts are repealed:(a) 1931 PA 285, MCL 125.31 to 125.45.(b) 1945 PA 282, MCL 125.101 to 125.115.(c) 1959 PA 168, MCL 125.321 to 125.333.(2) Any plan adopted or amended under an act repealed under subsection (1) is subject to section 81(1). Act 342 of 2010 SIGNS ADVERTISING SEXUALLY ORIENTED BUSINESSES AN ACT to regulate the use of signs advertising sexually oriented businesses; to provide for the powers and duties of certain state and local governmental officers and entities; to provide remedies; and to prescribe civil sanctions.
