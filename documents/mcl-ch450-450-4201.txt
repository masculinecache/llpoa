CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.4201: 

450.4201 Sec. 201.A limited liability company may be formed under this act for any lawful purpose for which a domestic corporation or a domestic partnership could be formed, except as otherwise provided by law. A limited liability company formed to provide services in a learned profession, or more than 1 learned profession, shall comply with article 9.
