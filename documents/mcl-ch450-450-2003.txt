CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2003: 

450.2003 Sec. 1003.A foreign corporation which transacts business in this state without a certificate of authority under this act is subject to the same duties, restrictions, penalties and liabilities now or hereafter imposed upon a foreign corporation which receives such certificate of authority, in addition to any other penalty or liability imposed by law.
