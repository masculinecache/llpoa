CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.63: 

554.63 Sec. 3.A right of termination under a terminable interest which was created prior to the effective date of this act is unenforceable if the specified contingency does not occur within 30 years after the terminable interest was created or within 1 year after the effective date of this act, whichever is later.
