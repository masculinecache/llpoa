CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.192: EXECUTIVE REORGANIZATION ORDER

450.192 Sec. 192.Saving clause. This act shall not impair or affect any act done, offense committed or right accruing, accrued, or acquired, or liability, penalty, forfeiture or punishment incurred prior to the time this act takes effect, but the same may be enjoyed, asserted, enforced, prosecuted or inflicted, as fully and to the same extent as if this act had not been passed.
