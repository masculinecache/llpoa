CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1491a: 

450.1491a Sec. 491a.As used in this section and sections 492a to 497:(a)"Derivative proceeding" means a civil suit in the right of a domestic corporation or a foreign corporation that is authorized to or does transact business in this state.(b)"Shareholder" means a record or beneficial owner of shares and includes a beneficial owner whose shares are held in a voting trust or held by a nominee on the owner's behalf.(c)"Disinterested director" means a director who is not a party to a derivative proceeding, or a director who is a party if the corporation demonstrates that the claim asserted against the director is frivolous or insubstantial.
