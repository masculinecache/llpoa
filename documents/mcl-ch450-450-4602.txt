CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.4602: 

450.4602 Sec. 602.A limited liability company shall amend its articles of organization if any of the following occur:(a)A change in the name of the limited liability company.(b)A change in the purposes of the limited liability company.(c)A change to or from the management of the limited liability company by managers.(d)A change in the maximum duration of the limited liability company.(e)A statement in the articles of organization has become false or erroneous, except that a change in registered office or resident agent may be made as provided for in section 209.
