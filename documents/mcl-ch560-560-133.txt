CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.133: 

560.133 Sec. 133.The caption of the final plat shall be printed at the top of the plat in large, bold letters, and shall include:(a)Name of the plat.(b)Part of section, number of section, town and range, municipality and county.(c)If a private claim, the number of the claim and the municipality in which the land is situated.(d)If a tract of land that is not a section or part of a section, the name by which the tract is legally known and the town and range and municipality in which the land is situated.
