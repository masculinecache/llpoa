CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.3849: 

125.3849 Sec. 49.(1) This act does not alter the authority of a planning department of a city or village created by charter to submit a proposed master plan, or a proposed extension, addition, revision, or other amendment to a master plan, to the planning commission, whether directly or indirectly as provided by charter.(2) Subsection (1) notwithstanding, a planning commission described in subsection (1) shall comply with the requirements of this act.
