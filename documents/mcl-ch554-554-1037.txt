CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.1037: 

554.1037 Sec. 27.This act modifies, limits, or supersedes the electronic signatures in global and national commerce act, 15 USC 7001 to 7031, but does not modify, limit, or supersede 15 USC 7001(c) or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in 15 USC 7003(b).
