CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.1040: MICHIGAN UNIFORM ASSIGNMENT OF RENTS ACT

554.1040 Sec. 30.This act takes effect 90 days after the date it is enacted into law. Act 115 of 2022 MICHIGAN UNIFORM ASSIGNMENT OF RENTS ACT AN ACT to enact the uniform assignment of rents act; to provide for the creation, perfection, and enforcement of security interests in rents; to provide remedies; and to repeal acts and parts of acts.
