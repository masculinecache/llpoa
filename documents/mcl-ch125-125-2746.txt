CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2746: 

125.2746 Sec. 6.Before placing vacant property into the program, the local governmental unit shall first offer the vacant property to owners of adjacent and contiguous property who occupy the adjacent and contiguous property. If adjacent and contiguous landowners do not purchase the property, the local governmental unit shall offer the vacant property to neighborhood resident organizations, other community groups, and the general public. The local governmental unit shall determine the sale price for any sale under this section.
