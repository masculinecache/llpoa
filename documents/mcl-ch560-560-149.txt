CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.149: 

560.149 Sec. 149.(1)A certificate shall be signed and dated by the majority of the county plat board, signifying its approval of the plat.(2)The certificate shall include a statement that the plat was reviewed for conformance to all applicable provisions of this act by the county plat board, by the county plat engineer, or both.
