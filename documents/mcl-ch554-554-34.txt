CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.34: 

554.34 Sec. 34.No remainder, valid in its creation, shall be defeated by the determination of the precedent estate, before the happening of the contingency on which the remainder is limited to take effect; but should such contingency afterwards happen, the remainder shall take effect in the same manner, and to the same extent, as if the precedent estate had continued to the same period.
