ARTICLE 31 EFFECTIVE DATE
Public Hearing having been held hereon, the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared to be necessary
for the preservation of the public health, peace and safety, and are hereby adopted by ordinance on May 13,
2003, by the Otsego County Board of Commissioners and become effective the day after approval by the
Michigan Department of Commerce.
