CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.208: 

560.208 Sec. 208.The sworn certificate of the surveyor who made the plat and, if a firm of surveyors also by a partner or principal officer, shall appear on the plat and shall state the following:(a)The name of the governing body by whose order the plat was made, and the date of the order.(b)A statement that the plat is a correct representation of all the exterior boundaries of the land surveyed and each parcel or lot thereof.(c)A statement that he has fully complied with the provisions of this act in filing the plat.
