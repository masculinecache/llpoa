CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.111: Revised Statutes of 1846

554.111 Sec. 1.The reversionary interest in lands conveyed on a condition subsequent may be granted, conveyed, transferred or devised by the owner of such interest, and by the subsequent grantees or devisees thereof, either before or after the right of re-entry becomes effective: Provided, That this act shall not affect any such interest created before it takes effect.
