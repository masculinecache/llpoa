CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.4: 

554.4 Sec. 4.When a remainder in fee shall be limited upon any estate which would be adjudged a fee tail according to the law of the territory of Michigan as it existed previous to the time mentioned in the preceding section, such remainder shall be valid as a contingent limitation upon a fee, and shall vest in possession, on the death of the first taker, without issue living at the time of such death.
