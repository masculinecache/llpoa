CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1472: 

125.1472 Sec. 72.(1) Except as provided in subsection (2), the term "consumer housing cooperative" shall be included as a part of the corporate name as set forth in the certificate of incorporation.(2)This section shall not apply to a corporation organized before March 10, 1967, as a nonprofit cooperative corporation pursuant to sections 98 to 132 of Act No. 327 of the Public Acts of 1931, as amended, being sections 450.98 to 450.132 of the Michigan Compiled Laws.
