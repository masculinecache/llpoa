CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.693: 

450.693 Sec. 3.When such proprietors shall be organized as a corporation in the manner hereinbefore provided, they shall have all the powers and privileges, and be subject to all the duties of a corporation, according to the provisions of chapter 55, so far as such provisions shall be applicable in such case, and not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter.
