CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2077: 

125.2077 Sec. 77.The fund shall appoint a higher education coordination council containing individuals representative of higher education institutions and having the necessary credentials to provide an inventory and advise the fund on the available research and development enterprises that are or will be established by higher education institutions and the application of that technology to business and industry.
