CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.4509: 

450.4509 Sec. 509.(1)A member may withdraw from a limited liability company only as provided in an operating agreement. A member withdrawing pursuant to an operating agreement may become entitled to a withdrawal distribution as described in section 305.(2)An operating agreement may provide for the expulsion of a member or for other events the occurrence of which will result in a person ceasing to be a member of the limited liability company.
