CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.605: 

554.605 Sec. 5.For the purposes of this act and any litigation arising thereunder, the security deposit is considered the lawful property of the tenant until the landlord establishes a right to the deposit or portions thereof as long as the bond provision is fulfilled, the landlord may use this fund for any purposes he desires.
