CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1289: 

450.1289 Sec. 289.(1) A professional corporation that is the surviving entity of a merger or conversion shall only have as shareholders licensed persons that are permitted to be shareholders under this chapter.(2) A professional corporation organized to provide services in a learned profession may merge with, or convert into, only other corporations or entities whose shareholders, members, partners, and managers, following the merger or conversion as applicable, are licensed persons permitted to be shareholders under this chapter. 450.1301.D10 CAPITAL STRUCTURE AND CORPORATE FINANCE Chapter
