CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.186e: 

450.186e Sec. 186e.Should any such corporation cease to operate or become unable to usefully serve the purpose of its organization, the legislature may by law provide for the winding up of its affairs and for the conservation and disposition of its property in such way as may best promote and perpetuate the purposes for which such corporation was originally organized.
