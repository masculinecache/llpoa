CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1491: 

125.1491 Sec. 91.Limited dividend housing association includes general or limited partnerships, limited liability companies, joint ventures, or trusts, as any such entities shall be approved by resolution of the authority. Members of a limited dividend housing association shall include each and all persons with a legal or beneficial interest of any kind in a limited dividend housing association or its assets.
