CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2706: 

450.2706 Sec. 706.(1) If a domestic corporation has not commenced business, has not issued any shares or memberships, and has not elected a board, the corporation may merge with any domestic or foreign corporation by unanimous consent of its incorporators.(2) If incorporators unanimously consent to a merger under subsection (1), a majority of incorporators shall execute a certificate of merger under section 707.(3) The other domestic or foreign corporations that participate in the merger with a domestic corporation under subsection (1) shall comply with the provisions of this act dealing with mergers that are applicable to them.
