CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.1013: 

554.1013 Sec. 3.(1) Except as otherwise provided in subsection (2), the court may issue an order under this act only after notice and opportunity for a hearing appropriate in the circumstances.(2) The court may issue an order under this act under the following circumstances:(a) Without prior notice if cause exists to require issuance of an order before notice is given.(b) After notice and without a prior hearing if cause exists to require issuance of an order before a hearing is held.(c) After notice and without a hearing if no interested party timely requests a hearing.
