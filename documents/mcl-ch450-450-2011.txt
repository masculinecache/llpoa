CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2011: 

450.2011 Sec. 1011.A foreign corporation shall not transact business in this state until it has procured a certificate of authority to transact business from the administrator. A foreign corporation may be authorized to transact business in this state that may be transacted lawfully in this state by a domestic corporation, to the extent that it is authorized to transact that business in the jurisdiction where it is organized, but no other business.
