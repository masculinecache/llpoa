CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.616: TRUTH IN RENTING ACT

554.616 Sec. 16.This act takes effect April 1, 1973 and applies only to security deposits held pursuant to leases entered into, renewed or renegotiated after April 1, 1973. Act 454 of 1978 TRUTH IN RENTING ACT AN ACT to regulate rental agreements for residential premises; to prohibit the inclusion by lessors of certain clauses or provisions in residential rental agreements; to require the disclosure by lessors of certain information; to require the inclusion of certain provisions in residential rental agreements; to regulate the commercial sale of printed rental agreement forms; and to prescribe penalties.
