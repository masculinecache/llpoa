CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.4208: 

450.4208 Sec. 208.(1)A resident agent of a limited liability company may resign as agent upon filing a written notice of resignation with the administrator and with a member or manager of the limited liability company.(2)The company shall promptly appoint a successor resident agent.(3)The appointment of the resigning agent terminates 30 days after the date the notice is filed with the administrator or upon the appointment of a successor, whichever occurs first.
