ARTICLE 29 CONFLICTING REGULATIONS AND
REPEAL OF PRIOR ORDINANCES
Whenever in the county there are provisions in two (2) or more laws or ordinances that have conflicting
provisions, the law or ordinance with the more stringent requirements or regulations shall govern, except
where a township has a validly enacted zoning ordinance under the provisions of Public Act 110 of 2006, the
Michigan Zoning Enabling Act, then for as long as such zoning ordinance remains lawful and in effect, its
regulations shall govern the use of land within that township.
