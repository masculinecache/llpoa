CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.164: 

559.164 Sec. 64.Conveyances and other instruments affecting title to any condominium unit in a condominium project shall describe the same by reference to the condominium unit number of the condominium subdivision plan and the caption thereof, together with a reference to the liber and page of the county records in which the master deed is recorded. The conveyances and other instruments are recordable.
