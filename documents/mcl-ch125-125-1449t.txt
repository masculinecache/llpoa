CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1449t: 

125.1449t Sec. 49t.Incident to the foreclosure of a mortgage pursuant to sections 49 to 49v, the authority or its agents or assigns may enter the mortgaged premises for the purpose of posting or serving the notices required by sections 49 to 49v.
