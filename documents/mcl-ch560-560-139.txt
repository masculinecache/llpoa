CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.139: 

560.139 Sec. 139.All public utility easements included in the plat shall be shown as follows:(a)By their widths and relationship to the lot or street lines.(b)As at least 12 feet wide where the rear lines of lots are contiguous.(c)As at least 6 feet wide if a lot has no adjoining subdivisions.
