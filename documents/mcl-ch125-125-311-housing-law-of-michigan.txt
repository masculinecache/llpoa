CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.311: HOUSING LAW OF MICHIGAN

125.311 Sec. 1.Whenever any portion of any township becomes an incorporated village, or whenever any territory is annexed to any village, the then existing zoning regulations for the territory within the newly incorporated village or within the territory attached to a then existing village shall remain in full force and effect for a period of 2 years after incorporation or annexation unless the legislative body of the village shall lawfully adopt other zoning regulations or ordinances.
