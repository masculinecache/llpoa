CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1448l: 

125.1448l Sec. 48l.In any forfeiture, foreclosure, or specific performance case based upon a mortgage on real estate or land contract held by the authority, the court may fix and determine the minimum price at which the real property covered by the mortgage or land contract may be sold at the sale under the forfeiture, foreclosure, or specific performance proceedings.
