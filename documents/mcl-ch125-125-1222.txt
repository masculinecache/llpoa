CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1222: 

125.1222 Sec. 2.As used in this act: (a)"Division" means the division of minority business enterprise.(b)"Department" means the department of commerce.(c)"Minority business enterprise" means a business enterprise that is owned or controlled solely by 1 or more socially or economically disadvantaged persons. The disadvantage may arise from cultural, racial, chronic economic circumstances or background, or other similar cause.
