CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.538: 

554.538 Sec. 18.A custodian, acting in a custodial capacity, has the rights, powers, and authority over custodial property that an unmarried adult owner has over his or her own property, but a custodian may exercise those rights, powers, and authority in that capacity only. This section does not relieve a custodian from liability for breach of section 17.
