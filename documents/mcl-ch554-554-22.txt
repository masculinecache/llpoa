CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.22: 

554.22 Sec. 22.When a remainder shall be limited to take effect on the death of any person without heirs, or heirs of his body, or without issue, the words "heirs" or "issues" shall be construed to mean heirs or issue living at the death of the person named as ancestor.
