CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.4409: 

125.4409 Sec. 409.A municipality may take private property under the uniform condemnation procedures act, 1980 PA 87, MCL 213.51 to 213.75, for the purpose of transfer to the authority, and may transfer the property to the authority for use as authorized in the development plan, on terms and conditions it considers appropriate. The taking, transfer, and use shall be considered necessary for public purposes and for the benefit of the public.
