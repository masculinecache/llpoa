CHAPTER 560: SUBDIVISION CONTROL ACT OF 1967 (Land Division)
MCL 560.167a: 

560.167a Sec. 167a.Within 10 days of receipt of the plat under section 161(2)(d), the state transportation department shall do 1 of the following:(a) Approve the plat and notify the proprietor of its approval.(b) Reject the plat and notify the proprietor directly, giving the reasons in writing. The commission shall send a copy of the letter of rejection to the chairperson of the county plat board.
