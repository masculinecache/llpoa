CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2350: LAND RECLAMATION AND IMPROVEMENT AUTHORITY ACT

125.2350 Sec. 50.Any state governmental entity or local government that exercises powers or performs duties under this act shall make publicly available a list of its powers and duties under this act. For the purposes of this section, a local government may utilize a list prepared by a statewide association.
