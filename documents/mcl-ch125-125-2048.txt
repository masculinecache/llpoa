CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2048: 

125.2048 Sec. 48.Loans, loan insurance, or loan guarantees provided from the local government assistance account may be on such terms and conditions as the fund, in its sole discretion, determines to be reasonable, appropriate, and consistent with the purposes and objectives of the fund and this chapter.
