CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.43: 

554.43 Sec. 43.Estates, in respect to the number and connection of their owners, are divided into estates in severalty, in joint tenancy, and in common; the nature and properties of which respectively, shall continue to be such as are now established by law, except so far as the same may be modified by the provisions of this chapter.
