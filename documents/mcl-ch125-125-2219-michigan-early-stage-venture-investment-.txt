CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2219: MICHIGAN EARLY STAGE VENTURE INVESTMENT ACT OF 2003

125.2219 Sec. 19.An authority which has completed the purposes for which it was organized shall be dissolved by resolution of the township board. The property and assets of the authority remaining after the satisfaction of the obligations of the authority shall belong to the township.
