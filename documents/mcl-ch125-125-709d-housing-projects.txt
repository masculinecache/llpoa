CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.709d: HOUSING PROJECTS

125.709d, 125.709e Act 268 of 1941 DEFENSE HOUSING PROJECTS 125.711-125.718 Repealed. 1980, Act 180, Imd. Eff. July 2, 1980. Act 162 of 1953 HOUSING PROJECTS AN ACT to authorize cities and incorporated villages or townships to acquire, own, operate, maintain and dispose of housing projects of the United States government or any agency thereof, and in so doing to comply with all conditions imposed pursuant to the applicable laws of the United States; and to authorize payments in lieu of taxes.
