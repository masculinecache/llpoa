CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.168: 

559.168 Sec. 68.An association of co-owners shall keep current copies of the master deed, all amendments to the master deed, and other condominium documents for the condominium project available at reasonable hours to co-owners, prospective purchasers, and prospective mortgagees of condominium units in the condominium projects.
