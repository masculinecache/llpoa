CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2261: 

125.2261 Sec. 31.A fund created in section 19 expires on the date determined by the board of the Michigan early stage investment corporation that established the fund, which date must be after January 1, 2030. Any money in the fund on expiration of the fund, subject to all outstanding debts and obligation of the Michigan early stage venture investment corporation being defeased and satisfied, must be deposited in the general fund of this state.
