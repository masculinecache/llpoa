CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.28: 

554.28 Sec. 28.When a remainder shall be limited to the heir, or heirs of the body of a person to whom a life estate in the same premises shall be given, the persons who, on the termination of the life estate, shall be the heir, or heirs of the body of such tenant for life, shall be entitled to take as purchasers, by virtue of the remainder so limited to them.
