CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1706: 

450.1706 Sec. 706.(1)A domestic corporation that has not commenced business, has not issued any shares, and has not elected a board may merge with any domestic or foreign corporation by unanimous consent of its incorporators.(2)In order to effect the merger, the majority of incorporators shall execute a certificate of merger in accordance with section 707.(3)The other domestic or foreign corporations participating in the merger shall comply with the provisions of this act dealing with mergers that are applicable to them.
