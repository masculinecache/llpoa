CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1761: 

450.1761 Sec. 761.As used in sections 762 to 774:(a)"Beneficial shareholder" means the person who is a beneficial owner of shares held by a nominee as the record shareholder.(b)"Corporation" means the issuer of the shares held by a dissenter before the corporate action, or the surviving corporation by merger of that issuer.(c)"Dissenter" means a shareholder who is entitled to dissent from corporate action under section 762 and who exercises that right when and in the manner required by sections 764 through 772.(d)"Fair value", with respect to a dissenter's shares, means the value of the shares immediately before the effectuation of the corporate action to which the dissenter objects, excluding any appreciation or depreciation in anticipation of the corporate action unless exclusion would be inequitable.(e)"Interest" means interest from the effective date of the corporate action until the date of payment, at the average rate currently paid by the corporation on its principal bank loans or, if none, at a rate that is fair and equitable under all the circumstances.(f)"Record shareholder" means the person in whose name shares are registered in the records of a corporation or the beneficial owner of shares to the extent of the rights granted by a nominee certificate on file with a corporation.(g)"Shareholder" means the record or beneficial shareholder.
