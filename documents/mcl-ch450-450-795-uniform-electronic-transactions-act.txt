CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.795: UNIFORM ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS ACT

450.795 Sec. 5.(1)A person shall not fraudulently procure or attempt to procure a contract under this act.(2)A person who knowingly violates this act is guilty of a felony, punishable by imprisonment for not more than 2 years, or a fine of not less than $5,000.00, or both.(3)A person found guilty of violating this act shall be barred from obtaining future contracts with the state.
