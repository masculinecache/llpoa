CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2451: 

125.2451 Sec. 1.(1)This act shall be known and may be cited as the "land reclamation and improvement authority act".(2)For the purposes of this act, the words and phrases defined in sections 2 and 3 have the meanings ascribed to them in those sections.
