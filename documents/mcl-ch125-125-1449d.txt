CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.1449d: 

125.1449d Sec. 49d.The sale shall be at public sale, between the hours of 9 a.m. and 4 p.m., at the place of holding the circuit court for the county in which the premises to be sold, or some part of them, are situated, and shall be made by the person appointed for that purpose in the mortgage, or by the sheriff, undersheriff, or a deputy sheriff of the county, to the highest bidder.
