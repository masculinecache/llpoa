CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2554: 

450.2554 Sec. 554.An action against a director, shareholder, or member for recovery upon a liability imposed by section 551 shall be commenced within 3 years after the cause of action accrues. An action under section 552 shall be commenced within 3 years after payment by the director to the corporation.
