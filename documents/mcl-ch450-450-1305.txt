CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.1305: 

450.1305 Sec. 305.(1)A subscription for shares made before or after organization of a corporation is not enforceable unless in writing and signed by the subscriber.(2)A subscription for shares of a corporation to be organized is irrevocable and may be accepted by the corporation for a period of 6 months, unless otherwise provided by the subscription agreement or unless all the subscribers consent to its revocation.(3)A contract with a corporation to purchase its shares to be issued is a subscription agreement and not an executory contract to purchase shares, unless otherwise provided in the contract.
