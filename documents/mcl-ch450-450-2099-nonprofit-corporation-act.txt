CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2099: NONPROFIT CORPORATION ACT

450.2099 Act 162 of 1982 NONPROFIT CORPORATION ACT AN ACT to revise, consolidate, and classify the laws relating to the organization and regulation of certain nonprofit corporations; to prescribe their duties, rights, powers, immunities, and liabilities; to provide for the authorization of foreign nonprofit corporations within this state; to impose certain duties on certain state departments; to prescribe fees; to prescribe penalties for violations of this act; and to repeal certain acts and parts of acts.
