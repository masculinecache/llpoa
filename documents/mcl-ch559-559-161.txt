CHAPTER 559: CONDOMINIUMS (Condominiums)
MCL 559.161: 

559.161 Sec. 61.Upon the establishment of a condominium project each condominium unit, together with and inseparable from its appurtenant share of the common elements, shall be a sole property subject to ownership, mortgaging, taxation, possession, sale, and all types of juridicial acts, inter vivos or causa mortis independent of the other condominium units.
