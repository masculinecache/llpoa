CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.2251: 

125.2251 Sec. 21.The fund manager shall file an annual report with the Michigan early stage venture investment corporation that includes an annual financial audit conducted by an independent auditor and any other financial information and documentation required by the Michigan early stage venture investment corporation to ensure the proper administration and investment of the fund.
