CHAPTER 125: PLANNING, HOUSING, AND ZONING (Planning Housing and Zoning)
MCL 125.961: 

125.961 Sec. 1.The final relocation of residents under urban renewal shall not occur until relocation of the occupants of such property in standard residential dwellings is assured by the condemning unit of government. This assurance shall include the opportunity to purchase or rent available standard residential dwellings in areas of their choice within the geographical boundaries of the unit of government acquiring the property within their reasonable means free from discrimination of any kind.
