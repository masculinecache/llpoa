CHAPTER 554: REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY (Real and Personal Property)
MCL 554.254: OIL, GAS, OR MINERAL LEASES

554.254 Sec. 4.The remedies herein provided and the duty herein created are in addition to the duty and remedies existing at common law. Act 81 of 1929 OIL, GAS, OR MINERAL LEASES AN ACT relating to the record, forfeiture and surrender of oil, gas and other mineral leases, providing a procedure therefor, and providing a penalty.
