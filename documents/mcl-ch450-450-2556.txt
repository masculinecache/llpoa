CHAPTER 450: CORPORATIONS (Corporations (Nonprofit Corp Act))
MCL 450.2556: 

450.2556 Sec. 556.If the corporation's articles of incorporation contain a provision authorized under section 209(e), then a claim for monetary damages for a volunteer director, volunteer officer, or other volunteer's acts or omissions shall not be brought or maintained against a volunteer director, volunteer officer, or other volunteer. The claim shall be brought and maintained against the corporation.
